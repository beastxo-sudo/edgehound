Method, verification, and honest caveats

Sampling. A scheduled job wakes at every 5-minute boundary, reads BTC's live direction 60 seconds before close, and records the real Polymarket order-book ask for that side. Only live order-book prices count — slower fallback quotes are excluded from every stat.

Settlement. Each bet is scored by Polymarket's own resolved outcome after the market settles. Binance (5m and 1m) and Coinbase act as cross-checks; when they disagree with Polymarket, Polymarket wins — that's the feed that pays — and the divergence is flagged. Exact ties and unconfirmable windows are excluded, never guessed.

Fillability. Since 2026-07-05 the lab snapshots the full order book on every signal: dollars available at the quoted price and within 3¢. This is the variable paper P&L cannot see — a quoted 40¢ ask with $6 behind it cannot absorb a real bet. The panel above fills in as snapshots accrue.

What this is not. Paper fills assume zero market impact and perfect timing. A statistically strong paper record (the z-score here is far past chance) is a necessary condition for a real edge, not a sufficient one. The fillability data decides which it is.
