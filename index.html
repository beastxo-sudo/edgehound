EdgeHound is a self-managed paper-trading bot. It scans every active market on a cycle, scores them across four signal engines, places 10–20 simulated bets a day ($100–$1000 each, sized by confidence), manages exits with targets / stops / max-hold, and reweights its own signals based on what actually wins. No real money ever moves — this is a live hypothesis tracker.
